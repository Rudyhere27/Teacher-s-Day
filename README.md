# Teacher-s-Day
# 🎓 Teacher's Day Tribute — RPS

> *"A small way of expressing our deepest gratitude to the mentors who shape who we are becoming."*

A digital, interactive memory book built for **Teacher's Day**, dedicated to the teachers, mentors, and leadership who guide us every single day. 

Presented with warmth and gratitude by **Jigyasa**.

---

### ✨ Features

* 📖 **Interactive 3D Book Experience:** Seamless notebook-style page-turn engine with keyboard (`←` / `→`), button, and mobile swipe navigation.
* 👨‍🏫 **Teacher Tributes:** Dedicated spotlight slides with personal messages for:
  * Respected Director Sir & Ma'am
  * Neel Kamal Sir *(Principal)*
  * Vishal Jaiswal Sir *(Vice-Principal)*
  * Shreya Ma'am *(Class Teacher)*
  * Ranjeet Singh Sir *(Biology Teacher)*
  * Tarun Sir *(History & Mentor)*
* 📌 **Live Real-time Pinboard:** Connected to Firebase Firestore where students and visitors can leave their own live sticky notes on the corkboard.
* 🎵 **Atmospheric Background Music:** Integrated nostalgic audio toggle on the bottom-left corner.
* 🎨 **Handcrafted Aesthetic:** Chalkboard tones, floating chalk dust animations, and handwritten typography.
